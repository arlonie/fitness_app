## 💡 Design Decisions Made

**Why Weekly Stats?**
- Motivating for 14+ year-olds (peer comparison, gamification)
- Real-time feedback on behavior (eating, exercise, hydration)
- Encourages daily app opening

**Why Color-Coded Features?**
- Visual learners (majority of teens) quickly identify features
- Color psychology (green=progress, blue=info, orange=action, red=nutrition)
- Better accessibility & brand recognition

**Why Golden Yellow Primary?**
- High contrast on deep purple (accessibility ✓)
- Energy/motivation associations
- Works well in dark mode without adjustment
